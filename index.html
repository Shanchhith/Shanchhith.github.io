Research

2026

  • Digitising the Times of India archives and industrial data using LLMs and OCR with Madhav Kumar (Assistant Professor, Harvard Business School)
    OCR and LLM-based pipelines to convert archival newspaper and industrial records into structured data.
  • Brand equity on Amazon Remote Research Associate, Stanford GSB, with Sridhar Narayanan
    Uses UCSD Amazon review data and Keepa data to test whether brand equity exists and at what level.

2025

  • Do human coaches produce more weight loss than AI coaches? Evidence from HealthifyMe Remote Research Associate, Stanford GSB, with Anuj Kapoor (University of Missouri), Puneet Manchanda (University of Michigan), and Sridhar Narayanan
    Reconstructed large-scale panel data and estimated the causal effect of human coaching on weight loss using propensity score matching (KD-trees, Mahalanobis distance) and OLS on the matched sample.
    Replication package
  • Shrinkflation detection pipeline Remote Research Associate, Stanford GSB, with Irina (Stanford GSB) and Sridhar Narayanan
    GPU-accelerated SKU matching over 500K+ packaged-goods products using embedding retrieval, cross-encoder scoring, and threshold calibration.
  • Desun Hospital: leadership in emergency care (case study) with Debjit Roy and Viswanath Pingali, IIM Ahmedabad
    Teaching case on how Desun Hospital built a leading position in emergency care.
  • Revealed preferences in the Indian marriage market, 1950–2010 Research Associate, IIM Ahmedabad · working paper in preparation
    OCR and LLM classification pipeline over sixty years of Times of India matrimonial classifieds, with structural analysis of revealed preferences across caste, education, and geography.
  • Debt collection regulation: a causal analysis Research Associate, University of Missouri
    Difference-in-differences and event study on firm-level call data, estimating the effect of regulatory limits on call frequency and collection outcomes.

2024

  • Health, climate, and hospital insurance claims (Aarogyasri) Research Associate, IIM Ahmedabad
    Linked 3 million+ universal health insurance claims (2007–2015) to SHRUG village identifiers using fuzzy string matching on village names, then merged NFHS survey data and ERA5 temperature data to study how health and weather affect claims.
  • Transparency and video ad performance Research Associate, University of Missouri
    Analysis of video ad click data to study how transparency affects click-through and view rates.
  • Model diversity and consumer choice Research Associate, University of Missouri and Harvard Business School
    Causal study of how the racial diversity of apparel models shapes buyer preferences, combining a conjoint experiment on Prolific with sales data from a large brand by model and product.
